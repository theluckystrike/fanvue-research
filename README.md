# Fanvue research

Shareable report: https://theluckystrike.github.io/fanvue-research/

Research date: 3 October 2026. Thirteen references, including eight Asian personas; this does not establish ten profitable Asian AI businesses. Social observations, dated third-party counts, identity gaps, activity samples and unknown fields are labeled separately.

The earnings audit contains attributable reports for Aitana and Aika. No independently verified Fanvue-only monthly earnings or profit was found for any reference. Reported amounts retain their dates and business scopes; unknown does not mean zero. The calculator is hypothetical.

`evidence/public/` contains minimal public observations and an earnings evidence summary. `evidence/platform_sources/` contains original fact summaries linked to official sources, rather than reproductions of full third-party articles. Raw provider responses, authentication data, private media and local research logs are excluded.

Public profile portraits are shown as research references. Third-party portraits and source material remain subject to their respective owners’ rights.

GitHub Pages serves the static root of the main branch. No external scripts, fonts, analytics or build dependencies are used.
