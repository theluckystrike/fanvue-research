# Platform economics evidence

Research date: 3 October 2026.

## Creator Terms & Conditions

Updated 19 September 2026. The standard creator share is 80%, with a 20% platform share. Applicable promotional arrangements can differ. Human creator onboarding and delivery obligations apply.

Source: https://legal.fanvue.com/creator-terms

## Creator Earnings & Payouts

Updated 22 April 2026. Earnings can come from subscriptions, paid content, messages and tips. Pending periods are normally seven days and may extend to 28 days. Withdrawal initiation is targeted within ten business days. Refunds and chargebacks affect realizable earnings; gross sales are not profit.

Source: https://legal.fanvue.com/creator-earnings-payouts

## Community Guidelines

Updated 14 June 2026. AI content needs prominent disclosure. Depicted personas must appear adult. Replication of a real person requires appropriate consent and verification. Public assets must satisfy the platform’s public-content requirements.

Source: https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines

## Subscription settings

The stated subscription range is $3.99–$100. Relative update label observed; no stable publication date. Prices offered to users can vary with promotions and localized display.

Source: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/7860744-how-do-i-set-or-change-my-subscription-price

## Pricing and retention guidance

Dated 10 August 2026. The platform suggests $9.99–$15 subscription pricing and discusses roughly 10% subscription revenue and 60% paid-message revenue in early months, plus 40% retention at 30 days and 25% at 90 days. These are platform aggregates without a published sample or AI-specific methodology; they are not predictions for the reference accounts.

Source: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16233102-subscription-pricing-retention-guide

## PPV strategy guidance

Dated 10 August 2026. PPV is separate from subscriptions. Suggested $10–$15 pricing is strategy guidance, rather than proof of completed purchases or a mandatory universal price.

Source: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16237634-ppv-media-a-creator-s-strategy-guide
